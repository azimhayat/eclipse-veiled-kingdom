# Core Cast Concept Review

Review date: 2026-09-04

Decision: accept all seven designs as direction-setting concepts. Do not treat them as final sprite sheets or publish them before character approval and production cleanup.

## Accepted

- **Ensemble:** one coherent Orun material language without making the cast look uniform. All five human silhouettes are recognisable at a glance.
- **Aren:** the existing V2 sheet remains the production anchor. Its sand-gold hood, blue compass and broken blade separate him clearly from the court characters.
- **Liora:** reads as an active reformer rather than a rescued princess. Her human and living-light forms preserve the same identity and costume. The back seal provides a usable reveal motif.
- **Mira:** age, warmth, burden and archival function are clear. The Last Lamp is visually distinct from Liora's cyan light.
- **Serath:** remains visibly human and controlled. The hollow ring and fine vermilion command geometry communicate institutional power without turning him into a monster.
- **Ysra:** practical veteran silhouette, strong reach-weapon language and restrained authority colour. Her resemblance in facial structure to Liora can support their shared past without implying kinship.
- **Warden of Dust:** commanded and restored states are immediately readable; the same guardian is preserved rather than replaced or killed. The Aren scale comparison supports believable staging.
- **Nameless Magistrate:** distinct from both Serath and the Warden. The blank name-slot, scales and route chains directly express Chapter II's civic-erasure theme.
- **Occupation soldiers:** the striker, spear guard, shield keeper and memory archer share one credible Orun uniform while remaining readable by weapon, mass and stance. Visible human faces support the story that these opponents are commanded citizens.
- **Veil paratrooper:** the folded, descending, landing and recovery states preserve one soldier identity. The cloth-and-bronze veil-wing belongs to Orun rather than modern military equipment.

## Production refinements before integration

- Simplify very fine gold filigree on Mira and Serath for gameplay-size rendering; preserve it for cinematics and Chronicle portraits.
- Increase visibility of the faded blue inner face of Ysra's divided cloak in her final production turnaround.
- Give Liora an early-fragment silhouette sheet that hides identifying facial detail while preserving her agency through posture and gesture.
- In the paratrooper production turnaround, anchor both open wing spars visibly to the shoulder-back frame so no pose can read as the soldier holding two independent gliders.
- Build transparent-background pose and animation sheets only after the user approves these silhouettes and emotional tones.
- Validate all characters beside the existing Aren sprite at actual desktop and phone gameplay scale before replacing or adding runtime art.
