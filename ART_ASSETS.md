# Original art assets

These images were created with the built-in ImageGen workflow for this project and copied into `public/assets`.

## `hero-sheet-v2.png`

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: production hero sprite sheet. The original sand-gold scavenger was refined into exactly six consistent full-body poses in a 3×2 layout: idle, run, jump, attack, dig, and climb. The upgraded protagonist is athletic and battle-worn, with layered leather-and-metal armor, a flowing hooded cloak, relic sword, glowing excavation tool, shadowed face, and subtle blue relic light. The final pass removed the background to genuine alpha while preserving the character, weapons, glow effects, pose positions, and clean edges.

## `title-still.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed 16:9 title still. Cinematic key art for “Eclipse of the Veiled Kingdom”: a cloaked scavenger in a sand-gold cloak digs into an earthen wall while holding a relic sword in a vast crumbling underground kingdom. Designed blocks, pressure plates, spikes, collapsing ledges, a radiant eclipse door, dropships, and parachuting soldiers fill a dramatic side-on 2.5D composition. Deep blue shadows, antique gold, sandy-orange sparks, volumetric light, realistic materials, and painterly dimensional forms. No text, logo, watermark, HUD, pixel art, Minecraft blocks, or isometric view.

## `kingdom-panorama.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide gameplay panorama. An immense underground ancient kingdom from a strict side-on platform-game perspective, with open painted cavern air, a dusky sinkhole sky, far ruined arches, mid-distance monumental pillars, chains, suspended dust, dunes, and glowing gold runes. Premium painterly materials and parallax-friendly layering in navy, indigo, antique gold, and sandstone orange. No characters, enemies, UI, text, watermark, opaque lower band, Minecraft dirt, pixel art, or isometric camera.

## `character-prop-atlas.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: character and prop production atlas. Separated side-view poses of one athletic hooded scavenger in a sand-gold cloak—idle, run, jump, sword slash, dig, and wall climb—plus parachuting/walking soldier poses, a dropship silhouette, relic diamond, pressure plate, push block, eclipse door, spikes, and crumble ledge. Polished hand-painted 2.5D assets with consistent lighting, readable silhouettes, deep-blue rim light, and warm antique-gold highlights. No labels, watermark, floor, overlap, or pixel art.
