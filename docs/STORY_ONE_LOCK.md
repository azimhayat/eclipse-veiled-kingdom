# Story One Narrative Lock

**Game:** Eclipse of the Veiled Kingdom  
**Story:** Story One — Eclipse of the Veiled Kingdom  
**Status:** LOCKED  
**Locked:** 2026-09-04  
**Scope:** 100 levels, ten chapters, one complete narrative

This document is the narrative authority for Story One. It overrides conflicting story, character, chapter, ending, or release-language in older planning notes. Level geometry and mechanic plans remain governed by their authored level files and campaign plan. Any material change to this story lock requires explicit user approval and a dated revision to this document.

## Player-facing structure

- Story One contains exactly **100 authored levels**.
- The story is divided into **ten chapters of ten levels each**.
- Each chapter takes place in a distinct realm. **Chapter** is the player-facing term; **realm** describes its location.
- V5 launches Story One with Chapters I–II, Levels 1–20.
- Chapters III–X release as reviewed ten-level additions, targeted monthly rather than promised on an unsafe fixed date.
- Unreleased chapters may appear only as named, locked **Coming Soon** destinations. They must not contain fake or placeholder levels.
- Completing the currently available chapters means the road continues; it must never claim that the kingdom or Story One is complete before Level 100.

## Story promise

> A forgotten cartographer awakens beneath an artificial eclipse and restores the stolen memories of a kingdom, only to discover that he helped build the machine that erased them—and that the lost princess he seeks chose to hide inside his broken map.

The player is not collecting treasure. The player is returning names, confronting the cost of perfect memory, and deciding whether remembering everything is justice, cruelty, or both.

## Canon before Level 1

The Kingdom of Orun built the **Crown Engine** to preserve every citizen's memories in living light. **Aren Vale**, a Crown Cartographer, designed routes that mapped those memories into the Engine. **Princess Liora of Orun**, heir to the kingdom and a reformer of its memory laws, worked with Aren to prevent the archive from becoming an instrument of rule.

During a succession crisis, **Regent Serath** inverted the Crown Engine. The artificial eclipse buried the capital, removed citizens' names from public history, and turned remembered duties into obedient hostile shades. Serath calls this forced forgetting mercy: a kingdom unable to remember pain cannot continue its wars.

Liora and Aren made one final countermeasure. With Liora's informed consent, Aren divided her living-light memory among the ten Crown Paths so Serath could neither erase her testimony nor use the heir as the Engine's master key. Liora's physical body was consumed during the inversion; what survives is a self-aware, fragmented continuity within Orun's memory network.

Aren erased his own memory to hide the paths. **Mira of the Last Lamp** preserved the final guiding flame. **Captain Ysra**, Liora's sworn protector and Aren's former ally, was led to believe that Aren caused Liora's death. The Eclipse settled over a kingdom that no longer remembered enough to resist.

## Locked main cast

### Aren Vale — the Veilbound

A skilled but vulnerable human hero, former Crown Cartographer, and participant in the Engine's creation. His lost memory protects the Crown Paths but also shields him from his responsibility. His journey moves from survival, to discovery, to accountability, and finally to an informed choice.

### Princess Liora of Orun — the Erased Heir

Liora is a co-protagonist, not a captive or reward. She opposed Serath, chose the fragmentation plan, challenges Aren when his restored memories become uncomfortable, and ultimately rejects an automatic return to hereditary rule. A restrained affection between Aren and Liora may emerge from their recovered history, but romance never replaces her agency or political purpose.

### Mira of the Last Lamp — guide and dissenter

A living archivist who speaks through sanctum flames. Mira wants Orun restored but conceals the Engine's cost and her role in placing Liora inside it. Her silence later in the story must feel like the consequence of withheld truth, not arbitrary betrayal.

### Regent Serath — the Hollow Crown

The principal antagonist. Serath knowingly uses erasure to create stability and obedience, but genuinely believes that forgetting pain is mercy. He must remain morally legible without being excused: consent, names, and choice are what his system removes.

### Captain Ysra — the divided oath

Former ally of Aren and sworn protector of Liora. Ysra hunts Aren because she believes he destroyed the princess. Recovering the truth breaks her loyalty to Serath, but it does not immediately erase her anger or the harm she committed for the occupation.

### The Choir Below — collective memory

The citizens stored in the Engine. The Choir is never one convenient voice. Its members disagree about restoration, release, revenge, identity, and whether the old kingdom deserves to return.

## Five-act narrative

1. **Remember the path — Levels 1–20:** Aren proves Orun existed, restores two Crown Paths, and recovers Liora's identity.
2. **Remember the crime — Levels 21–50:** Aren discovers how the Engine extracted, refined, and edited human memory.
3. **Remember the self — Levels 51–70:** Ysra, Liora, and Mira force Aren to confront what his maps enabled.
4. **Choose what survives — Levels 71–90:** the dead, the occupation, and the rebellion demand incompatible futures.
5. **Break the perfect memory — Levels 91–100:** Aren enters the Engine, defeats its imposed certainty, and chooses Orun's future.

## Princess Liora reveal cadence

Liora must be present from the beginning without being fully explained too early.

- **Levels 1–2:** an unidentified voice and a second cartographer symbol suggest Aren was not alone.
- **Level 3:** the Broken Procession shows an unnamed heir opposing Serath.
- **Level 4:** the original protective law bears the heir's seal before its corruption.
- **Level 7:** Mira admits the Last Lamp was kept for the lost heir.
- **Level 9:** the inward gate contains both Aren's mark and Liora's concealed seal.
- **Level 10:** the Warden confirms that Aren carried the heir's memory into the Crown Paths.
- **Levels 11–17:** fragments reveal her face, voice, civic work, and connection to Ysra without yet restoring a complete identity.
- **Level 18:** the dead speak the name **Liora**.
- **Level 19:** citizens rally around her testimony rather than her title.
- **Level 20:** Liora becomes a coherent living-light person and confirms that she consented to Aren's plan.
- **Levels 21–90:** Liora grows from recovered witness to active partner while repeatedly refusing to become a symbol others control.
- **Levels 91–100:** she stands beside Aren, confronts the consequences of their shared plan, and participates in—but does not dictate—the final choice.

## Cinematic lock

Story One uses **twelve primary cinematics**, not separate opening and closing movies for every chapter.

1. **Opening prologue:** Orun, the Crown Engine, Serath's inversion, the fall into Eclipse, and Aren's awakening. Liora remains an unnamed silhouette.
2. **Chapter I introduction:** Mira's call, Aren's broken sword, the impossible sunrise memory, and the first buried Crown Path.
3. **After Level 10 — Chapter I to II bridge:** the Warden is restored, the heir is revealed as erased, and the Inner Kingdom opens.
4. **After Level 20 — Chapter II to III bridge:** Liora coheres, reveals Aren's fragmentation plan, and Serath addresses the Cartographer directly.
5. **After Level 30 — Chapter III to IV bridge:** the water archive reveals memory being diverted toward the foundry.
6. **After Level 40 — Chapter IV to V bridge:** Aren sees how his routes fed extraction; a preserved face calls from the glass gardens.
7. **After Level 50 — Chapter V to VI bridge:** Mira's role is exposed and Ysra receives proof that Liora persists.
8. **After Level 60 — Chapter VI to VII bridge:** Liora stops the duel, Ysra withdraws with divided loyalty, and the false sky becomes the next target.
9. **After Level 70 — Chapter VII to VIII bridge:** Aren's complicity is made undeniable, Mira falls silent, and the dead open their hidden road.
10. **After Level 80 — Chapter VIII to IX bridge:** Liora rejects automatic rule while citizens choose to rise under their own names.
11. **After Level 90 — Chapter IX to X bridge:** Ysra opens the final road and Serath reveals the Eclipse is also containing catastrophic grief.
12. **After Level 100 — final cinematic:** the selected fate of Orun, the Choir, Aren, Liora, Mira, Ysra, and Serath.

The chapter bridge closes the completed chapter and introduces the next, avoiding two back-to-back videos. Cinematics must be skippable, subtitled, replayable from the Chronicle, excluded from completion time, and loaded only when needed. Skipping a cinematic never skips gameplay or story-state rewards.

## Ending lock

Level 100 presents three morally serious outcomes. None is labelled simply good or evil.

- **Restoration:** return every preserved memory and accept the renewed grief, conflict, and responsibility that follow.
- **Release:** free the stored dead, allow parts of old Orun to end, and build without complete historical continuity.
- **Synthesis:** dismantle the Crown's ownership of memory while preserving voluntary individual testimony; the hardest path because it requires the broadest restoration and strongest trust.

Liora does not automatically become queen in any ending. Orun's future government belongs to its restored living citizens. Aren cannot erase his guilt, and Serath's defeat cannot make the harm disappear.

## Delivery rules

- Most story is delivered through architecture, restored spaces, short dialogue, and optional evidence—not constant exposition.
- Mandatory in-level memory echoes should generally last three to eight seconds and never interrupt dangerous movement.
- Every chapter must reveal one major truth, change one important relationship, visibly restore part of Orun, and end with a distinct guardian or climax.
- Princess Liora is never kidnapped as the campaign objective, sexualized as a reward, or stripped of agency to motivate Aren.
- Aren is responsible but not secretly malicious; Serath is understandable but not vindicated; Mira is compromised but not casually treacherous; Ysra's change must be earned.
- The final choice cannot appear meaningful only in dialogue. Earlier discoveries, restored citizens, and character trust must alter its presentation and consequences.

## Continuity work required before V5

The current V4 review build deliberately combines the strongest earlier levels. Several Levels 11–20 still carry names and environmental identities reserved for later chapters, including the Sundered Aqueduct, Buried Foundry, Gardens of Glass, Hollow Barracks, Observatory, Sepulchre, siege, and throne.

Before V5 is called story-canonical:

- Reframe Levels 11–20 as Inner Kingdom districts using the locked Chapter II names and revelations.
- Preserve accepted gameplay wherever possible, but remove story language that prematurely consumes Chapters III–X.
- End Level 20 with the Nameless Magistrate or a narratively equivalent civic guardian, not the final Throne of the Eclipse.
- Replace whole-kingdom completion language with Chapter II completion and the Chapter III Coming Soon bridge.
- Integrate the twelve locked Liora moments for Levels 1–20 and the four V5 cinematics before claiming the narrative is present in play.

V4 remains a valid playtest build. This lock defines the story-facing work that separates it from V5.
